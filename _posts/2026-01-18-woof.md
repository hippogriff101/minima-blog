### So what is Woof?

Woof is a YSWS program that I ran at the start of this year. If you are not familiar YSWS stands for You Ship We Ship. [Hack Club](hackclub.com) runs events regularly where you _ship_ (publish a project) and then Hack Club will send you something in return. YSWS at Hack Club are sponsored by a member of the Hack Club staff, most likely a Gap Year / Intern.

__Basically: you build something, and Hack Club sends you cool stuff.__

Woof is based on a YSWS ran previously in December 2025 called [Meow](meow.hackclub.com). __You Ship__ 5 hours of coding on a cat related project and __We Ship__ a cat plushie. I wanted to apply that premise to dogs!

### Getting a sponsor

As I said above, getting a HQ sponsor is a very important part of a YSWS. They are your Point of Contact for Hack Club HQ and get you the budget (moneyy), domain and handle fulfilment from HQ as well as lots more.

There are many ways to get a sponsor. Most commonly is getting a large number of RSVP's. This can be gained through regularly posting in #ysws.

There is also cold DMing. This is not a recommended technique but I gave it a go. I DMed @AVD (Alex Van Doren) who is interning at Hack Club on Christmas Day if he would sponsor Woof. Alex also sponsored Meow that i spoke about above.
